Core Features

Everything You Need to Raise and Track Complaints

Submit complaints, track their progress in real time, receive updates, and manage your grievance journey through a simple and secure digital portal.

Unique Complaint ID

Every submission generates a trackable ID so you can monitor progress anytime, anywhere.

Auto Routing

Your complaint is automatically forwarded to the concerned department for quick action.

Real-Time Tracking

View live status updates, timeline history, officer remarks, and expected resolution dates.

Complaint History

Access all your previous complaints, monitor their progress, and review complete resolution timelines whenever you need.

Anonymous Complaints

Submit grievances anonymously when you prefer privacy while still receiving a tracking ID.

Notifications

How It Works

Four simple steps from complaint submission to resolution.

  1. 01

    Register & Login

    Create your citizen account or log in to access the portal securely.

  2. 02

    Submit Complaint

    Fill in details, upload evidence, and receive your unique Complaint ID instantly.

  3. 03

    Auto Assignment

    The concerned department reviews your complaint and begins the resolution process.

  4. 04

    Track & Resolve

    Monitor status updates through the timeline until your complaint is resolved and closed.
